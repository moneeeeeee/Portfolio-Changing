Neck Braces in Mountain Biking and Motocross (in progress)

Co-author | Literature Review, Injury Biomechanics

Overview

A team literature review on whether neck braces prevent neck and spinal cord injury, written for UBC's open injury biomechanics textbook.

Neck braces are widely sold to mountain bikers and motocross riders, but how strong is the evidence that they prevent cervical spine and spinal cord injury?

Tools

  • Literature review
  • Medline
  • injury biomechanics

Role

Co-author. UBC injury biomechanics course, team project (Sept – Dec 2026).

Description

  • Searching medical and engineering databases for experimental, computational and epidemiological studies.
  • Evaluating the methods used in the field and the injury criteria they rely on.
  • Identifying controversies and gaps to prioritize future research.
  • Writing a peer-reviewed section for the course's open online textbook.

Outcome

This page will be updated with the findings when the review is complete.
