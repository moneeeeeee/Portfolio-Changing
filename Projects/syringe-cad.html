Syringe CAD Model & Technical Drawings

Mechanical design | SolidWorks, Technical Drawings, Assembly Modelling

Overview

A syringe modelled from scratch in SolidWorks, with technical drawings for every part and for the assembly.

A medical device is only manufacturable if its drawings are complete and unambiguous. This assignment was practice in taking a familiar device from individual part models to a documented assembly.

Tools

  • SolidWorks
  • Part and assembly modelling
  • Technical drawings

Role

Mechanical design. UBC biomedical engineering design course.

Description

  • Modelled each syringe component from scratch as a separate SolidWorks part.
  • Combined the parts into a full assembly model.
  • Produced technical drawings for the individual parts and for the assembly.
  • Applied the same workflow, models plus drawings, to the MRI-compatible infusion pump and the single-cell aspiration system projects.

Outcome

The exercise gave me a repeatable CAD workflow that I reused on two team device projects, and a clearer sense of how much a drawing has to say for someone else to build the part.
