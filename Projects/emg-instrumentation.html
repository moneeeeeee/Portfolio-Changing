Biosignal Instrumentation Labs

Individual lab work | MATLAB, Arduino, LTSpice, EMG / ECG

Overview

EMG-controlled servo, an EMG amplifier designed in LTSpice, ECG and PPG heart-rate detection, and ultrasound image processing.

A series of labs following physiological signals from the body to a usable output: acquiring them, conditioning them in analog and digital form, and turning them into measurements or control signals.

Key Numbers

20–500 HzEMG front-end passband
4th-orderButterworth band-pass
60 Hzmains notch

Tools

  • MATLAB

  • BioRadio

Role

Individual lab work. UBC biomedical instrumentation course.

Description

  • Recorded bicep EMG and processed it in MATLAB: band-pass, 60 Hz notch, rectification and low-pass envelope extraction.
  • Streamed EMG into MATLAB in real time and drove LEDs and a servo motor through an Arduino in proportion to contraction strength.
  • Designed and simulated an EMG front end in LTSpice: instrumentation amplifier, active filters, summing amplifier and precision rectifier scaling the output to 0 to 5 V.
  • Recorded ECG, PPG and oxygen saturation together and wrote a peak-detection heart-rate method, checked against a pulse oximeter at rest and under cuff occlusion.
  • Integrated spirometer flow data to calculate tidal volume and vital capacity.
  • Enhanced ultrasound images with median filtering, intensity adjustment and sharpening, and acquired B-mode, Colour Doppler and elastography images on phantoms.

Outcome

Seeing the same signal from both the circuit side and the software side made me comfortable moving between them.
