EEG Signal Acquisition & BCI Analysis

Lab work | EEG, Signal Processing, BCI Software

Overview

Calibrated a 14-channel EEG headset, analysed four frequency bands and demonstrated real-time neurofeedback with trained mental commands.

How well can a consumer-grade EEG headset capture brain activity across different mental and physical states, and can it be trained to recognize simple commands?

Key Numbers

14EEG channels
100%electrode contact quality
4frequency bands analysed

Tools

  • 14-channel EEG headset and its BCI software

Role

Lab work. UBC.

Description

  • Calibrated the headset to full contact quality across all electrodes before recording.
  • Recorded under varied cognitive and physical states and analysed alpha, beta, theta and gamma band activity.
  • Identified artifacts such as eye blinks and jaw tension in the recordings.
  • Trained mental-command and facial-expression recognition using the headset's BCI software and demonstrated real-time neurofeedback.

Outcome

The lab gave me practical experience with biosignal calibration and with how sensitive EEG is to contact quality and artifacts.
