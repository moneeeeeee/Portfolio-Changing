Cancer Genomics & Multi-Omics Analysis

Data analyst and pipeline developer | R, DESeq2, ggplot2, TCGA

Overview

An R pipeline on TCGA kidney cancer data: patient matching across data types, mutation clustering, survival analysis and pathway enrichment.

Do patterns of somatic mutation relate to survival in clear cell renal cell carcinoma? The data covered more than 500 patients but was spread across clinical, mutation and RNA-seq files with inconsistent identifiers.

Key Numbers

500+patients in the TCGA cohort
354patients with complete, matched data
50most-mutated genes used for clustering
3data types aligned

Tools

  • R
  • tidyverse
  • DESeq2
  • survival
  • survminer
  • ggplot2
  • TCGA
  • cBioPortal

Role

Data analyst and pipeline developer. UBC course project, team of three.

Description

  • Wrote R scripts that parse TCGA barcodes, standardize patient identifiers and remove duplicates, leaving a core cohort of 354 patients with clinical, mutation and RNA-seq data.
  • Clustered patients on binary mutation profiles of the 50 most frequently altered genes using Ward.D2 hierarchical clustering.
  • Used PCA to check that the subgroups were distinct before comparing them.
  • Compared outcomes between clusters with Kaplan-Meier survival analysis.
  • Ran differential expression with DESeq2 and pathway enrichment, which pointed to disrupted metabolic and immune-signalling pathways.
  • Produced survival curves, mutation heatmaps and pathway plots in ggplot2 for the team's report.

Outcome

The pipeline is modular and reproducible. The main lesson was that most of an analysis is decided in the matching and cleaning steps that come before any statistics.
