Inclusive Design for Cataplexy (in progress)

Team member | User Research, Interview Design, Inclusive Design

Overview

A team inclusive-design project on writing tools for people who live with cataplexy, the sudden loss of muscle tone that occurs in narcolepsy.

Cataplexy episodes can be triggered by strong emotion and may cause a person to lose their grip without warning. The project asks how everyday writing tools could be designed with these users in mind, starting from what already exists and from what end users say they need.

Tools

  • Literature and product research
  • End-user interview design
  • Inclusive design methods

Role

Team member. UBC professionalism and ethics in biomedical engineering course, team project (Sept – Dec 2026).

Description

  • Researching existing solutions for writing tools and where they fall short for this group.
  • Drafting the five end-user interview questions the team will use.
  • Contributing to the team's project plan.

Outcome

This page will be updated with the team's findings and design direction when the project is complete.
