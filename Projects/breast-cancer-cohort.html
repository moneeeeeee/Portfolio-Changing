Metastatic Breast Cancer Cohort Study

Contributing analyst (author 6 of 13) | R, ggplot2, Survival Analysis, DESeq2

Overview

Co-author of an ASCO 2026 abstract on real-world outcomes and tumour gene expression in 43 patients treated with antibody-drug conjugates.

Antibody-drug conjugates have changed care for metastatic breast cancer, but there are few biomarkers that predict who will respond. The study examined outcomes and genomic correlates of response in patients who had whole genome and transcriptome sequencing.

Role

Contributing analyst (author 6 of 13). Research study at BC Cancer and Canada's Michael Smith Genome Sciences Centre (2025 – 2026).

Description

  • Prepared clinical and treatment data for the cohort in R, turning report-based information into analysis-ready tables.
  • Produced figures and survival summaries used by the team to compare outcomes across treatment groups.
  • Supported the differential expression and immune-cell analyses alongside the lead analysts.
  • Learned the cohort workflow end to end, from DNA and RNA libraries through variant, expression and RNA quality-control data.

Outcome

The work was presented at the 2026 ASCO Annual Meeting (J Clin Oncol 44, suppl 16, abstr 1034). Working with real-world clinical data that was not designed for analysis taught me to document every cleaning decision, because the interpretation depends on it.
