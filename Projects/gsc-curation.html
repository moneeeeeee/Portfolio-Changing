Cancer Variant Evidence Curation

Curator | CIViC, GraphKB, Clinical Trials, FDA Records

Overview

Curated 131 evidence statements into the public CIViC knowledgebase and co-authored the CIViC 2026 paper in Nucleic Acids Research.

Clinicians interpreting a tumour genome rely on knowledge bases that link variants to therapies and trials. Those entries are only useful if each one is accurate and traceable to its source.

Role

Curator. Co-op work at Canada's Michael Smith Genome Sciences Centre (BC Cancer) (Sept 2025 – Apr 2026).

Description

  • Read oncology publications in detail and wrote concise evidence statements covering disease context, biomarker, therapy, endpoints, sample size and strength of evidence.
  • Reviewed clinical trials from 3 registries, curating biomarker-based trials into the GraphKB knowledge base and recording the rest in a trial sheet.
  • Cross-checked biomarker interpretation and trial eligibility across publications and regulatory records before submitting entries.
  • Created a tracking sheet for the review status of every paper and statement, so future curators can continue the work.
  • Interviewed team members about how they use GraphKB and proposed improvements to search, filtering, examples and documentation.

Outcome

The statements are now public, reusable evidence for variant assessment. I am a co-author of the CIViC 2026 update paper (Nucleic Acids Research), credited for data curation and manuscript review. The work taught me to treat traceability as part of the result.
