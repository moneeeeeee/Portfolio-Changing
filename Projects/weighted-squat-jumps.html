Weighted Squat Jump Aiding Device (design concept)

Mechanical design contributor | SolidWorks, Technical Drawings

Overview

A team design concept for a device that standardizes depth and tempo in weighted squat jumps for training and rehabilitation.

Weighted squat jumps are used in training and rehabilitation, but depth and tempo vary from one repetition to the next, which makes progress hard to compare.

Tools

  • SolidWorks
  • technical drawings

Role

Mechanical design contributor. UBC team design concept (not built).

Description

  • Modelled the mechanism in SolidWorks and produced technical drawings.
  • Proposed claw-style grips for plate stability and adjustable rails to fit different body sizes.
  • Specified how sensors could capture displacement, velocity and repetition count in a future build.

Outcome

The design was not built or tested. Its value was in the reasoning: balancing biomechanics, ergonomics and manufacturability on paper.
